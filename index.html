Get to know Episodes

Less organizing. More watching.

Pick up where you stopped

Mark episodes and seasons watched, and jump straight to the next unwatched episode. Star favourites and archive finished shows.

Find it on your services

Choose your watching country and filter your saved library by streaming service. Availability is provided by JustWatch via TMDB and may differ by season.

Keep the useful details

Browse series, season and episode scores, summaries, and cast. Previously downloaded information remains available offline.

Keep your own history

Your library lives on your device. Export a backup to a location you choose and restore it when you move to another phone.

A few things worth knowing

Can I watch episodes in the app?

No. Episodes keeps track of what you watch; it does not play, stream, or download TV episodes. Streaming services require their own subscriptions.

What does the purchase include?

The planned Play purchase includes unlimited series and all app features. There is no ten-series limit or in-app unlock. Fresh metadata and artwork require internet access and depend on third-party services.

Do I need another account?

No Episodes account is required. Google Play uses your Google account for the purchase and reinstallation. Android cloud backups, if enabled, are handled by your device's backup service.

Already using the original Episodes?

This is an independently maintained fork of Episodes, originally created by Jamie Nicol and contributors. It installs separately from the original F-Droid app. Read how to transfer your library.
