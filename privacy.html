Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026. Applies to Episodes for Android, application ID io.github.ebisbe.episodes.

Pre-release policy. The app is maintained by ebisbe. The publisher's legal identity and dedicated privacy/support email will be added before the Play release.

Your library stays on your device

Episodes stores your saved shows, watched-episode progress, notes, favourites, archived status and preferences in the app's private storage. Downloaded metadata and artwork are also cached locally. The app does not operate an Episodes account system or upload your library to a developer-operated server.

There are no advertising, behavioural analytics, or remote crash-reporting SDKs in the current app. Episodes does not request GPS or other device-location access: you choose your watching country manually.

Requests to TMDB and its image service

To search for shows and download or refresh information, the app connects directly to The Movie Database (TMDB) and its image delivery service. Requests can contain your search text, show/season/episode identifiers and the selected metadata language. Streaming availability is obtained from TMDB and displayed for your selected country; this integration does not make direct requests to JustWatch.

As with other internet requests, these services receive your IP address and request information. Requests identify shows whose information you are accessing, but do not include your local watched flags or personal notes. Automatic refresh can make requests in the background when enabled. You can change its interval, restrict it to unmetered networks, or disable it in Settings.

These third parties process requests under their own terms and privacy practices; data may be processed outside your country. See TMDB's privacy policy. Fresh metadata and images require these network services; already saved information can be used offline.

Backups and device transfer

When you export a backup, Android's document picker lets you choose the destination. The backup includes your library and personal notes. If you choose a cloud-storage provider, that provider handles the file under its own privacy policy. Episodes does not separately encrypt exported library files, so keep backups somewhere you trust.

The app also allows Android's system backup and device-transfer features to include the library database and preferences. Whether this happens depends on your device, backup provider and settings. Derived caches are excluded by the app's backup rules. Check your Android backup settings to manage system backups and their retention.

Google Play purchases

The planned Play release is paid upfront. Google Play processes payment, refunds, and purchase history under Google's privacy policy. The app does not collect payment-card details, contain an in-app purchase SDK, or send purchases to a developer-operated verification server. Google may make sales and financial reports available to the publisher through its merchant tools.

Deleting your information

You can remove individual shows in the app. Clearing app storage in Android Settings or uninstalling removes the app's local library, preferences and caches. That does not automatically delete backup files, copies held by your backup provider, or records held independently by Google Play or TMDB. Manage those with the respective provider. There is no Episodes account to delete.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact the publisher, the information you choose to share is used to answer your request. Do not post backup files, private notes, account details or payment information in public issues. Dedicated email contact details and support-data retention information will be published here before launch. General product questions can be raised through the support page.

This website and external links

The supplied site contains no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies set by its own code, third-party fonts, or contact forms. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub receives request information including visitors' IP addresses for hosting and security; see GitHub's privacy statement. Opening external links uses your browser and the destination's own privacy practices.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app's data handling changes. The date above identifies the latest revision. Publisher identity, contact details and the final policy must be completed before the paid release.
